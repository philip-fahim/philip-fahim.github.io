About Farah Khuzouz, LMFT

Your story deserves to be understood in its full context.

I'm Farah Khuzouz, a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist (LMFT) in New York State and the therapist behind Stories in Context. I offer online therapy to couples, families, and individuals — including adolescents, international students, and immigrants — located anywhere in New York State.

I work with people navigating relationships, identity, cultural background, life transitions, anxiety, depression, trauma, and intergenerational patterns. My approach is rooted in empathy and narrative therapy: the belief that you are the author of your own story, and that your culture, family, and relationships are essential context — never a footnote.

My professional experience includes university counseling centers, outpatient clinics, crisis centers, school-based settings, and foster care. My goal is to create a warm, culturally responsive space where couples, families, and individuals can navigate challenges and find resilience together.

Who I Work With

Culturally responsive therapy for Arab, Middle Eastern & SWANA communities

Stories in Context was built for people whose culture, language, and faith are central to who they are. My Approach

What is narrative therapy?

Narrative therapy is a collaborative, respectful approach that sees you as the author of your own story — not as a problem to be fixed. The name Stories in Context comes from this idea: that the way you understand yourself is shaped by the personal, cultural, and family narratives you carry.

In plain terms, we slow down and look at the stories you've come to believe about who you are and what's possible. We gently separate you from the problem, notice the values and strengths that are already there, and work together to retell the chapters that no longer fit. Your culture, family, and relationships aren't background details — they're the context that makes your story make sense.

Culturally responsive by design

As a culturally responsive Marriage and Family Therapist, I hold space for the languages, values, faith, and family systems you come from. Sessions are available in English and Arabic, and you're always the expert on your own life and culture.
